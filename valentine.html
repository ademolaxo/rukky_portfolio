VAL"26

CHOWDECK [Valentine 2026] Check Site ↗

For Valentine's Day 2026, Chowdeck set out to make the brand part of the romance — positioning itself as the customer's playful “Secret Lover” while building a marketplace and experience around gifting, connection, and celebration. The campaign aimed to drive 100K orders while bringing Valentine's into the product, marketing, and physical world. I owned the visual direction for the product and campaign website, as well as the illustrated materials used throughout the campaign. I also supported the wider creative team across social and physical marketing, helping extend the visual language beyond the digital experience. The core challenge was to create a visual world that felt distinctly Valentine's without becoming a collection of disconnected seasonal assets — and to make illustration work as part of the experience, not just decoration.

Collaborators
Role: Illustrator · Art Direction
Company: Chowdeck
Team: Design x Product
The idea : Your Secret Lover

The campaign imagined Chowdeck as the attentive lover who is always checking in, sending surprises, and making customers feel cared for.

The idea also extended beyond romantic relationships, exploring the different ways people give and receive love.

Building the visual world

The campaign website was designed as an illustrated tabletop, with everyday objects becoming portals into different Valentine's experiences.

A crystal ball opened the matchmaking experience. A chocolate box led to a food soulmate quiz. A Walkman became an entry point to the Valentine's playlist, while a handwritten note opened the Wall of Letters.

I developed the visual direction around these objects, creating a playful and cohesive world that could hold multiple experiences without feeling visually fragmented.

Illustration as interface

The illustrations weren't designed simply as decoration. Each object had a role within the experience and needed to communicate that it could be interacted with.

The visual system was designed with interaction in mind — from the glowing crystal ball and moving chocolate wrapper to the playing Walkman and flickering candle.

This allowed the illustration itself to become part of the navigation and storytelling of the website.

A Valentine's experience across the product

The Valentine's experience extended beyond the campaign website and into the Chowdeck app itself

Working closely with the Product team, I helped shape the visual direction for the customised Valentine's experience — from the customised vertical section and order sequence through the gifting flow and checkout. We also created a custom pink app icon, giving the Valentine's experience a presence from the moment customers opened the app.

The goal was to make Valentine's feel integrated into the product rather than simply layered onto it, carrying the campaign's visual language through the key moments of discovering, ordering, and gifting.

Extending the world

The same visual language was carried into the wider Valentine's campaign through illustrated marketing materials, social media, and physical campaign touchpoints.

As a supporting designer, I worked with the creative team to adapt and extend the visual direction across these different formats, keeping the campaign recognisable wherever customers encountered it.

The result

The final experience brought together illustration, interaction, and campaign storytelling to make Valentine's feel like a part of the Chowdeck brand — rather than simply another seasonal promotion.

It was an opportunity to use illustration and visual design not only to create a distinctive campaign identity, but to shape how customers discovered, ordered, gifted, and experienced Valentine's through Chowdeck.
