Office Branding

CHOWDECK

As Chowdeck moved into a new office, I worked on translating the brand from something experienced on a screen into something people could physically walk through, interact with and remember. I led the environmental branding across the space, from a 16 × 8 ft glass mural to wayfinding, signage, icons and smaller branded touchpoints

Collaborators
Role: Environmental designer. Art direction
Company: Chowdeck
A new creation

The centrepiece started with a Renaissance painting. Inspired by Michelangelo’s The Creation of Adam, I reimagined the iconic reaching gesture through the world of Chowdeck. Instead of giving life to man, the exchange became about making everyday life easier — connecting people to food, groceries and essentials.

The result became a visual world filled with the things people experience through Chowdeck, brought together in a single, larger-than-life composition.

Building a branded environment

The mural became the starting point for a wider environmental system. I developed wayfinding around Chowdeck’s signature street-sign asset, using colour-coded navigation across floors, alongside a custom icon set and room signage throughout the office.

I also designed branded graphics across mirrors and architectural surfaces, including a custom “Meeting in Progress” sign created specifically for the space.

The details make the place

Individually, these are small moments. Together, they make the workplace feel cohesive and unmistakably Chowdeck. The same visual language moves from a large-scale mural to the signs people rely on to find a room, creating consistency across very different scales and functions.

A brand you can experience

Designing at this scale reinforced something I believe about branding: it doesn't stop at a logo or a campaign. A strong brand can shape the spaces people move through, the things they notice and even the small interactions they barely think about.

For Chowdeck, the office became another place for the brand to show up — not just as something to look at, but as something to experience.
