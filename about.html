I work in the space between a good idea and how people experience it — across illustration, brand design, and increasingly, art direction.

My practice spans developing visual identities and illustration systems, campaigns, digital products, environmental branding, and physical experiences. I care about the small, weird details nobody asks for but everyone remembers, and how the whole thing at scale feels intentional.

At Chowdeck, I've led illustration for the past year while working across the wider brand, building visual direction, running major campaigns and experiences, and now mentoring and managing a junior designer. That's pushed me toward shaping ideas and setting direction, not just executing them.

Illustration sits at the centre of my work and now, it's alongside a growing practice in brand design and art direction.
