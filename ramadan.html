Holy Month

CHOWDECK [RAMADAN AND LENT CAMPAIGN]

Ramadan and Lent coincided for the first time in nearly three decades, giving Chowdeck an opportunity to speak to two communities through one seasonal campaign. I led the art direction, illustration and asset development for the campaign, contributing to the early visual exploration and helping shape how the brand could approach both communities with cultural specificity.

Collaborators
Role: Illustrator · Art Direction. Brand design
Company: Chowdeck
Team: Design
A Feast For The Season

Rather than relying on generic religious symbolism, the creative direction started with something both communities have in common: food, gathering and sharing.

The idea of a communal feast became the foundation for the visual world. Abundant meals, familiar Nigerian dishes and the act of sharing food became a way to represent generosity, togetherness and the rituals surrounding Ramadan and Lent.

Starting with what we share

For Ramadan, I helped shape a rich visual direction inspired by Muslim communities across Nigeria, with saturated colours, geometric forms, crescent moons, lanterns and food associated with Suhoor and Eid.

For Lent, the direction shifted to a cooler, more restrained palette, with imagery rooted in faith, sacrifice and reflection — including the cross, candles, dove and palm leaves.

Two worlds, one campaign

The two worlds were intentionally distinct, while remaining connected through a shared Chowdeck visual language.

Food became more than supporting imagery. I illustrated individual dishes and brought them together into larger compositions that could flex across different campaign messages and formats.

Illustration as the visual language

The visual system extended beyond campaign communications into Chowdeck's in-app Ramadan experience.

Ramadan Mode, the Ramadan Corner and Iftar-focused messaging were built around the daily 5–8pm breaking-fast window, with free delivery and curated meal discovery helping make ordering during Iftar easier.

The illustrations and visual language brought the campaign into the product itself, creating a more cohesive seasonal experience across Chowdeck.

The result

The campaign brought Ramadan and Lent together through a shared visual idea: the joy of food, faith and coming together.

Rather than creating a collection of seasonal assets, we built two distinct visual worlds within one campaign framework — giving each community its own cultural language while allowing Chowdeck to remain recognisable throughout.
